It repeats back what it is unsure of. Speech recognition is markedly worse on rural dialects, women's voices and code-mixed speech than benchmarks suggest. A transcription below threshold is confirmed before anything is recommended on the strength of it.
It asks what she wants separately. Current trade and desired trade are different questions. Inferring the second from the first is how a system hands hereditary occupations back to people.
Caste is never an input to a recommendation. It determines eligibility for the scheme and nothing else.

The first answer is a certificate, not a course

Recognition of Prior Learning already exists. It gives NSQF-aligned certification to people who learned by doing, and it takes days rather than months. It is also almost unknown to the people it was built for, so this is what Hunar leads with.

Her nine years of plastering already correspond to this level. She is not an unskilled worker who needs six months of training. She is an uncertified skilled worker who needs an assessment.
Every card is retrieved, never generated. A language model asked to suggest a qualification returns a plausible name, code, level and duration, and a share of them do not exist. A woman sent to a centre that is not there loses a day of wages and does not come back. So the model only parses what she said. Recommendations are looked up in the National Qualification Register and carry its real identifiers. When nothing matches, Hunar says it does not know and hands off to the block office.
